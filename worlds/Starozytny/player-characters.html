Description

Tired, chaotic medic. Very efficient, but kind of weird.

An orange scaled Kobold, distant from her herritage and closer to death than to the living.

Backstory

Rher left the northern clans of Purrnia—having learned the medical trade—in search of a better life, a decision she would come to regret.
Driven by a thirst for knowledge and prestige, she used her medical expertise to secure a place in the military.

Unlike other medics, she is tasked with clearing away the remains of soldiers who fell in battles along the empire's borders. She retrieves bodies by crawling across the battlefield after the fighting ends, her mission being to cover up the inhumane experiments to which Purrnia's soldiers were subjected.
She pursues her scientific curiosity with the freedom her prestige affords her.

She made the mistake of coming into contact with large quantities of the compound used in the experiments on the soldiers, causing deformities in her scales and a heaviness in her limbs. Her mutations progress slowly, wearing her down until she becomes a liability to the operation's continuity.
Shortly thereafter, she is exiled.

Officially, she is sent on a diplomatic mission from which she is unlikely to return, alongside other expendable individuals.

The members of this party recognize their situation and know they will not return.
In solidarity with Rher's health issues, her companion Seda shares a prosthetic to aid the mobility of her failing leg.

One night, while suffering from her usual insomnia, the group comes under attack. Rher does not react; she does not defend herself. She wakes up in a river of bodies—her companions dead.
Still dazed, she carries out her usual procedure. She takes the remains of her friends and walks. She walks until she collapses from exhaustion.
